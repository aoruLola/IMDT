对于给定的各解释变量  $ x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{k} $ 的一组具体值  $ X_{0} = (1, x_{10}, x_{20}, \cdots, x_{k0}) $，由

 $$ y_{i}=\beta_{0}+\beta_{1}x_{1i}+\beta_{2}x_{2i}+\cdots+\beta_{k}x_{ki}+\varepsilon_{i}=X_{i}\beta+\varepsilon_{i},\quad i=1,2,\cdots,n $$

有

 $$ \hat{y}_{0}=\hat{\beta}_{0}+\hat{\beta}_{1}x_{10}+\hat{\beta}_{2}x_{20}+\cdots+\hat{\beta}_{k}x_{k0}=X_{0}\hat{\beta} $$

其中

 $$ x_{i}=\left(1,x_{1i},x_{2i},\cdots,x_{ki}\right),\quad\beta=\begin{pmatrix}\beta_{0}\\ \beta_{1}\\ \vdots\\ \beta_{k}\end{pmatrix} $$

用回归值 $ \hat{y}_{0} $作为 $ y_{0} $的估计值时，它们的预测误差为

 $$ \boldsymbol{e}_{0}=\boldsymbol{y}_{0}-\hat{\boldsymbol{y}}_{0}=\boldsymbol{X}_{0}\boldsymbol{\beta}+\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_{0}-\boldsymbol{X}_{0}\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}=\boldsymbol{X}_{0}(\boldsymbol{\beta}-\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})+\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_{0} $$

因为  $ y_{0} $ 与  $ \hat{y}_{0} $ 均服从正态分布，所以  $ e_{0} $ 也服从正态分布，而且

 $$ \begin{aligned}\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{e}_{0})&=\boldsymbol{E}\big[\boldsymbol{X}_{0}(\boldsymbol{\beta}-\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})+\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_{0}\big]\\&=\boldsymbol{X}_{0}\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{\beta}-\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})+\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_{0})=0\end{aligned} $$

 $$ \begin{aligned}D(\hat{y}_{0})&=E[\hat{y}_{0}-E(\hat{y}_{0})]^{2}=E[X_{0}\hat{\beta}-X_{0}\beta]^{2}\\&=E[X_{0}(\hat{\beta}-\beta)X_{0}(\hat{\beta}-\beta)]\\&=E\{X_{0}(\hat{\beta}-\beta)[X_{0}(\hat{\beta}-\beta)]^{\prime}\}\\&=E[X_{0}(\hat{\beta}-\beta)(\hat{\beta}-\beta)^{\prime}X_{0}^{\prime}]\\&=X_{0}E[(\hat{\beta}-\beta)(\hat{\beta}-\beta)^{\prime}]X_{0}^{\prime}\\&=X_{0}\mathrm{Cov}(\hat{\beta},\hat{\beta})X_{0}^{\prime}\\&=\sigma^{2}X_{0}\left(X^{\prime}X\right)^{-1}X_{0}^{\prime}\\ \end{aligned} $$

 $$ \begin{aligned}D(e_{0})&=D(y_{0}-\hat{y}_{0})=D(y_{0})+D(\hat{y}_{0})\\&=D(\varepsilon_{0})+D(\hat{y}_{0})=\sigma^{2}+\sigma^{2}X_{0}(X^{\prime}X)^{-1}X^{\prime}_{0}\end{aligned} $$

即

 $$ e_{0}\sim N(0,\sigma^{2}(1+X_{0}(X^{\prime}X)^{-1}X_{0}^{\prime})) $$

构造统计量

 $$ \begin{aligned}&t=\frac{\frac{y_{0}-\hat{y}_{0}}{\sqrt{\sigma^{2}(1+X_{0}(X^{\prime}X)^{-1}X_{0}^{\prime})}}}{\sqrt{\frac{\frac{ee^{\prime}}{\sigma^{2}}}{n-k-1}}}\\ &=\frac{y_{0}-\hat{y}_{0}}{\hat{\sigma}\sqrt{1+X_{0}(X^{\prime}X)^{-1}X_{0}^{\prime}}}\sim t(n-k-1)\\ \end{aligned} $$

其中  $ \hat{\sigma} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} e_{i}^{2}}{n-k-1}} $.

对于给定的置信度  $ 1-\alpha $，查 t 分布表可得临界值  $ t_{\frac{\alpha}{2}}(n-k-1) $，令

 $$ P\{\mid t\mid<t_{\frac{\alpha}{2}}(n-k-1)\}=1-\alpha $$

即

 $$ P\{-t_{a/2}(n-k-1)<t<t_{a/2}(n-k-1)\}=1-\alpha $$

得  $ y_{0} $ 的置信度为 1-α 的预测区间为

 $$ (\hat{y}_{0}-t_{\frac{\alpha}{2}}(n-k-1)\hat{\sigma}\sqrt{1+X_{0}(X^{\prime}X)^{-1}X_{0}^{\prime}},\hat{y}_{0}+t_{\frac{\alpha}{2}}(n-k-1)\hat{\sigma}\sqrt{1+X_{0}(X^{\prime}X)^{-1}X_{0}^{\prime}}) $$
